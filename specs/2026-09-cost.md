# Spec: what a closure costs

- **Feature list entry**: #15 — "`progress.mjs cost` reports, per closing commit on the first-parent line, the runs recorded and the commits made since the previous closure, the quick and red runs among them, and the totals per closure; `--since <date>` narrows it, `--json` gives the rows"
- **Author / session date**: 2026-09-16
- **Status**: approved — the second of the three fast-lane debts left after 0.2.0

---

## Problem

The fast lane was asked for on a number: about eight gate runs and three
commits per closure, counted by hand from `verify-log/` and `git log` on
2026-09-16. Nothing in the harness counts it, so nobody will know whether
spike branches, the one-commit closure and the quick run changed it, or
notice when it drifts back. A cost nobody measures is a preference.

## In scope

- `node scripts/progress.mjs cost [--ref <ref>] [--since <date>] [--json]`:
  the closing commits on the first-parent line of `ref` (`HEAD` by default)
  — a commit whose `feature_list.json` has an entry passing that its first
  parent did not have passing, or had not at all — oldest first; for each,
  the runs under `verify-log/` recorded after the previous closure and up to
  this one, with the quick and the red ones counted, and the commits (no
  merges) in the same span; then the runs and commits since the last
  closure; then totals and per-closure figures (mean and median).
- `--since <date>` keeps closures on or after the date; `--json` prints the
  rows and totals as one object.
- The report reads the record: `readRuns()` of `verify-log.mjs` for the runs,
  `git log --first-parent` and `git show <commit>:feature_list.json` for the
  closures, `git rev-list --no-merges` for the commits. It writes nothing.

## Out of scope

- Attributing a run to a closure by anything but time. A run's file says
  when it started and which commit it was based on, not which entry it was
  for; spans between closures are the honest cut.
- A threshold. The number is reported, not judged.
- The upgrade command: the last debt, next.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

- **AC1** (ubiquitous) — `closingCommits(ref)` shall list, oldest first, each
  first-parent commit whose `feature_list.json` closes at least one entry
  against its first parent, with the commit, its committer date and the ids
  closed; a commit with no `feature_list.json` on either side closes nothing.
- **AC2** (ubiquitous) — `cost({ ref, since })` shall give one row per
  closing commit with `runs`, `quick`, `red` and `commits` counted in the
  span since the previous closure's date up to and including this one's, a
  `pending` row for what came after the last closure, and totals with
  `runsPerClosure` and `commitsPerClosure` as mean and median.
- **AC3** (event) — When `--since <date>` is given, the rows before the date
  shall be left out and the totals recomputed over what is left.
- **AC4** (ubiquitous) — The command shall print one line per closure with
  the short commit, the date, the ids, and the four counts, then the pending
  line and the totals; `--json` shall print the same as JSON.
- **AC5** (ubiquitous) — On this repository the command shall run and report
  at least the closures of #10 to #14 with a run count of at least one each.
